# Aniraku Terms of Service

**Revision date:** August 13, 2026

These terms describe the relationship between Aniraku’s hosted experience, open-source Android client, account features, community tools, and third-party sources. They are written to be readable and should be reviewed before creating an account or posting content.

## 1. The service

Aniraku is an open-source anime discovery and playback client. It uses AniList and other public metadata sources to help people find titles, view episode information, track progress, maintain bookmarks, and participate in community features.

Aniraku does not represent itself as the owner, studio, distributor, or official streaming service for anime titles displayed in the interface. It does not intentionally host a permanent library of episode files. Playback availability depends on third-party sources and can vary by title, device, region, and time.

## 2. Accounts, sync, and local data

Most browsing is available without an account. An account is required for cloud-backed features such as synchronized watch history, ratings, bookmarks, comments, profile information, and optional external-library synchronization. Users are responsible for protecting their credentials and for activity carried out through their account.

Guest progress and bookmark information may be retained locally. Local records do not automatically follow a user to another device until the relevant feature has synchronized through an authenticated account. Account deletion is intended to remove service-controlled profile data; browser storage, backups, logs, public posts, and third-party providers can have independent retention rules.

## 3. Community conduct

Comments, replies, profiles, ratings, and other user submissions must be lawful, relevant, and respectful. Do not submit harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hate, sexual material involving minors, doxxing, malware, spam, impersonation, copyright-infringing uploads, or instructions intended to compromise another person or service.

Users retain ownership of submitted material but grant Aniraku the limited permission needed to store, display, moderate, and technically distribute it through the community features they use. Do not submit material without permission to share it.

## 4. Third-party metadata and playback

Anime metadata, artwork, descriptions, scores, airing information, and relationships are supplied by third-party providers, primarily AniList. Playback resolution can use public third-party sources or configured provider routes. Those services control their own availability, content, policies, and retention.

Titles, thumbnails, source labels, and metadata can be inaccurate, incomplete, stale, geo-restricted, or unavailable. They do not constitute an endorsement by Aniraku. Report suspected unsafe or misleading records through the available issue or content-report paths.

## 5. Reports and availability

Use the service DMCA and content-report route for copyright notices. Community reports can be reviewed by maintainers or moderators, who may hide, remove, restrict, or preserve content where reasonably necessary to protect users, comply with law, investigate abuse, or maintain the service.

The service is provided on an **as available** basis. Features may change, pause, or be discontinued; third-party failures can prevent playback or metadata access; and maintenance or abuse protection can temporarily restrict requests. Use Aniraku only where both its use and the underlying third-party sources are lawful.
