01 · SEEK

Double-tap left for −10s, right for +10s. Long-press holds 2× speed.

02 · SWIPE

Vertical swipe: brightness on the left, volume on the right. Scrub the timeline to jump.

03 · SUBTITLES

English subtitle tracks with a high-contrast overlay, spoiler-safe discussion below.

04 · SOURCES

Direct first, proxy fallback, then automatic provider switch. Sub and Dub servers side by side.

05 · SLEEP

Sleep timer with presets from 15 minutes to 2 hours. Fires once, then rests.

01Is this app completely free?+

Yes — fully free and open source. No paywalls, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

02Do I need an account?+

No. Download and start watching immediately. Sign in only to sync your library and history.

03Does this track my data?+

No. Zero analytics, zero telemetry, zero tracking. Your data stays on your device.

04What devices are supported?+

Android 9.0 and above. Grab the universal APK if you're unsure — it covers ARM64, ARM32, and x86_64.

05Why isn't this on the Play Store?+

Google prohibits open-source anime streaming apps. We distribute directly via GitHub Releases for full transparency.

06Is this legal?+

Aniraku is an open-source media player connecting to publicly accessible sources. It hosts no content. Consult your local laws regarding third-party streaming.

07Can I contribute?+

Yes — pull requests, issues, and discussions are welcome on GitHub.
