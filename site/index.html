WATCH

Watch
your way.

It works the way your hands expect: tap to pause, swipe for volume, double-tap to skip. You'll never open the help page.

Gesture Controls Picture-in-Picture Custom Subtitles Sleep Timer
01 · SEEK

Double-tap left to jump back 10 seconds, right to jump forward. Hold your finger down for 2× speed.

02 · SWIPE

Slide up the left edge for brightness, the right edge for volume. Drag the bar to scrub.

03 · SUBTITLES

English tracks included where available, drawn big and readable over the picture.

04 · SOURCES

Sub and Dub servers listed side by side. If the stream dies, the app tries the next option by itself.

05 · SLEEP

Falling asleep to an episode? Set the timer from 15 minutes to 2 hours and it'll pause for you.

INSTALL ANDROID 9.0+
  1. 01

    Download the APK — Universal works on every phone. Only pick ARM64/ARM32 if you know you need the smaller file.

  2. 02

    Allow unknown sources — Android will ask. It's under Settings → Security → “Install unknown apps”.

  3. 03

    Install — open the file from your notifications and tap through.

  4. 04

    Open & watch — that's genuinely it. Sign in later if you want your list synced.

DOCUMENTATION

Read the docs.

Short answers to the stuff people actually ask.

01Getting Started→ 02Privacy Policy→ 03Terms of Service→ 04DMCA→ 05Source Code→ 06Contributing→
01Is this app completely free?+

Yep. No price, no premium tier, no "free*" asterisk. If you ever pay, it's a donation because you felt like it.

02Do I need an account?+

Nope. Install it and watch. An account only matters if you want your watch history and library synced.

03Does this track my data?+

No analytics, no telemetry, nothing phoning home about what you watch. Check the source if you don't believe it.

04What devices are supported?+

Anything on Android 9 or newer. Not sure which chip your phone has? Just take the universal APK.

05Why isn't this on the Play Store?+

Google doesn't allow this kind of app. Direct downloads from GitHub mean you always know exactly what you're installing.

06Is this legal?+

Aniraku doesn't host a single video — it's a player that reaches public sources. Whether watching them is fine where you live is on you, so check your local laws.

07Can I contribute?+

Please do. Bug reports, translations, code — it all lives on GitHub and every bit helps.
