WATCH

Watch
your way.

It works the way your hands expect: tap to pause, swipe for volume, double-tap to skip. And when a stream dies, the player rotates servers before you notice.

Gesture Controls Picture-in-Picture Custom Subtitles Sleep Timer
01 · SEEK

Double-tap the left side to jump back 10 seconds, the right side to jump forward. Hold your finger down for 2× speed.

02 · SWIPE

Slide up the left edge for brightness, the right edge for volume. Drag the bar to scrub.

03 · SUBTITLES

Pick the track, then set font, size, background opacity, outline thickness and offset. Preferences stick between episodes.

04 · SOURCES

Sub and Dub servers side by side, each remembering its own quality. Four fallback layers rotate if a stream won't start.

05 · ANISKIP

Intro and outro markers are detected automatically, with a skip button on the video — no more 90-second openings.

06 · SLEEP

Timer presets from 15 minutes to 2 hours, with a countdown pill on the video. It pauses itself while you doze off.

01Is this app completely free?+

Yep. No price, no premium tier, no "free*" asterisk. If you ever pay, it's a donation because you felt like it.

02Do I need an account?+

Nope. Install it and watch. An account only matters if you want your watch history and library synced.

03Does this track my data?+

No analytics, no telemetry, nothing phoning home about what you watch. Check the source if you don't believe it.

04What devices are supported?+

Anything on Android 9 or newer. Not sure which chip your phone has? Just take the universal APK.

05Why isn't this on the Play Store?+

Google doesn't allow this kind of app. Direct downloads from GitHub mean you always know exactly what you're installing.

06Is this legal?+

Aniraku doesn't host a single video — it's a player that reaches public sources. Whether watching them is fine where you live is on you, so check your local laws.

07Can I contribute?+

Please do. Bug reports, translations, code — it all lives on GitHub and every bit helps.

08Do downloads actually work offline?+

Yes. Downloads are saved on your device and play with no connection at all. You can cap storage at 1, 2 or 5 GB (or leave it unlimited) and lock downloads to Wi-Fi only. Progress is tracked to the byte, and interrupted downloads resume instead of starting over.

09What is AniSkip?+

Intro and outro markers come from the stream's own chapter data plus AniSkip, matched to the episode you're watching. When an opening or ending starts, a skip button appears on the video with a countdown — one tap and you're past it. If a show has no timing data, the button simply never shows up.

10Can I import my MyAnimeList or AniList library?+

Yes. In Settings → Library Sync, connect either account to import your list and watch progress, or push your Aniraku progress back out. Import matches titles against Aniraku's catalog and tells you exactly what came across, what was already there, and what had no match.

11Why didn't I get a notification for a new episode?+

Notifications only fire for shows you've added to your list, and only after the episode is actually playable — we check the stream before sending anything. On Android 13 and newer you also have to allow notifications for Aniraku, and battery savers sometimes hold them back. Delivered alerts always land in Library → Alerts, so nothing is ever lost.

12Can I watch on my TV?+

Use Android's built-in screen mirroring or cast your whole phone to the TV — that works today. A dedicated cast button inside the app isn't planned, because screen mirroring already covers it without another moving part.

13How do I update to a new version?+

Settings → Application has a CHECK button that tells you when a newer build exists, and the Releases box above always links the current one. From v5.8.0 or newer the APK installs straight over the previous one, so your library and history stay put. Coming from V5.7.9 or older — including the old aniraku.anine.app alpha — uninstall it first; Android treats those as separate apps.

14How do I delete my account and data?+

Settings → Delete account wipes your profile, watch history, ratings, bookmarks, comments, notifications and preferences in one go. No account? Clearing history and saved titles in Settings removes what's stored on the device.

15Why is NSFW content hidden?+

It's off by default. Turning it on in Settings asks you to confirm you're 18 or older, and it only changes what the catalog search asks for — the preference itself is stored on your device. Turn it back off whenever you like.
