Aniraku is an anime discovery and playback app. Browse and search a large catalog, bookmark titles, track your episode progress, and watch with a gesture-driven player.

Highlights:

• Stream with quality selection (Auto, 1080p, 720p, and other available renditions)
• Download episodes in-app at a pinned quality — the video, segments, and subtitles are saved to private app storage and play back with no network
• Offline playback straight from the library — no external browser or export step
• Continue Watching rail, watch history, and per-episode ratings
• Bookmarks with new-episode notifications and airing reminders
• Comments per episode, spoiler-tagged
• Skip intro support, autoplay next episode, playback speed control, picture-in-picture
• Dark, distraction-free "Afterimage" interface with a red accent

Aniraku does not host any video. Sources come from third parties and availability can change. The app is open source under the MIT license.
