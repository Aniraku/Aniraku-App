# Copyright and DMCA Procedure

Aniraku respects intellectual-property rights and responds to valid copyright notices. If a rights holder believes material accessible through an Aniraku service infringes copyright, the rights holder or an authorized representative may write to **dmca@aniraku.tech**.

| `INCLUDE` | `DETAIL` |
| --- | --- |
| Signature | A physical or electronic signature from the authorized person. |
| Work identified | Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed. |
| Material location | A specific URL, title, or identifier sufficient to locate the challenged material. |
| Contact details | Name, organization where relevant, mailing address, telephone number, and email address. |
| Good-faith statement | A statement that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or law. |
| Accuracy statement | A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and the notifier is authorized to act. |

Aniraku may remove or disable access to material when appropriate and may apply a repeat-infringer policy consistent with applicable law. If you believe material was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice containing information required by applicable law. This procedure is not legal advice.
