# Aniraku Copyright and DMCA Notice Procedure

Aniraku respects intellectual-property rights and responds to valid copyright notices. If a rights holder believes that material accessible through an Aniraku service infringes copyright, the rights holder or authorized representative may send a notice to **dmca@aniraku.tech**.

| Required notice element | Description |
|---|---|
| Signature | Physical or electronic signature of the authorized person. |
| Work identified | Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed. |
| Material location | A sufficiently specific URL, title, or identifier to locate the challenged material. |
| Contact details | Name, organization where relevant, mailing address, phone number, and email address. |
| Good-faith statement | A statement that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or law. |
| Accuracy statement | A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and the notifier is authorized to act. |

Aniraku may remove or disable access to material when appropriate and may apply a repeat-infringer policy consistent with applicable law. Users who believe that material was removed in error may submit a counter-notice containing the information required by applicable law. This procedure does not constitute legal advice.
